  • Google Play Games is optionalIf you sign in, Google keeps your achievements, leaderboard scores and an optional cloud copy of your progress.

1. Who we are

Kakuro is developed and published on Google Play by Mar Dev. For anything about this policy or your data, write to mardevlopm@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Kakuro saves a file in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains:

  • your progress: solved levels, stars, best times, statistics, daily streaks, badges, lessons and the puzzle in progress;
  • your settings, such as language, theme, sound and accessibility options;
  • in-game counters, such as your hint balance and how many ads you've seen today, so that ad limits work.

This file is not sent to us. It stays on your device unless you choose to make a backup (section 7) or sign in to Google Play Games (section 6). It contains no name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

3. What we collect

We, Mar Dev, do not collect, store or sell personal information. The game has no sign-up with us, no analytics of our own and no servers of ours.

Like every developer on Google Play, we may see aggregated reports from Google: for example install counts, ratings, crash and performance reports (Android vitals) and ad performance summaries in AdMob. These reports do not tell us who you are. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to answer you.

5. Ads and your choices

  • Reward videos are always optional. You choose to watch one, for example to get hints. Full-screen ads only appear between puzzles, never while you are solving.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change it any time in Settings → Your progress → Ad privacy options (shown when it applies).
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Content. The game asks Google to keep ads suitable for a general audience (content rating "PG").

6. Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games on your phone, Kakuro signs you in automatically. You can also sign in from Settings → Google Play Games. When you are signed in:

  • Achievements (the game's badges) and your progress toward them are saved to your Play Games profile.
  • Leaderboards receive your total stars, longest daily streak, Daily Challenge time and fastest solve times. Your gamer name and avatar appear next to your scores. Who can see them depends on your Play Games profile settings.
  • Saved games: a copy of your game progress (the same data as in section 2) is stored in your Google account, so you can restore it on another phone.

You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in the Google Play Games settings and in your Google Account. If you don't sign in, none of this is sent.

7. Backups and puzzle codes

  • Backups are made only when you ask: Back up progress saves a file to your phone's Documents or Downloads folder, or copies a backup code to your clipboard. Where it goes after that is up to you.
  • Challenge codes and class sets only describe puzzles. They contain no personal information, and students need no account to use them.
  • Worksheets you export are saved as files on your device.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to check the connection, load ads and use Google Play Games.
  • Vibration: for small haptic feedback (can be turned off in the game).
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): lets Google use the advertising ID as described in section 5.

Kakuro does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or phone calls.

9. Children

Kakuro is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been shared through the game, contact us at mardevlopm@gmail.com and we will help you, including by contacting Google where needed.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: your progress stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (Android Settings → Apps → Kakuro → Storage → Clear data) removes it.
  • Google Play Games: kept in your Google account until you delete it there.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules. See How Google retains data.
  • Emails to us: kept only as long as needed to help you. Ask us and we'll delete them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, Morocco's Law 09-08 on the protection of personal data, and US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are handled directly in your device settings or your Google Account (sections 5, 6 and 10). You can always write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves. Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob as described above.

12. Security and international transfers

Your game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Network requests made by the game use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and its date. For important changes, we will also say so in the game's update notes on Google Play.
